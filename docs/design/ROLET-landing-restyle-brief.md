# ROLET landing restyle brief

Samet wants the landing to feel closer to two games we already like — not a copy of either. This file is the art + copy lock for that pass. No code. No 3D. No casino words. Do not write “roulette” anywhere on the page.

Locked assets stay: table v3, paper duelist A (2D card), HawkEye / Blocker / Healer v2. Palette and type from stil rehberi v1.2.

---

## 1. What to steal (concrete)

### Buckshot (chamber game — do not name the casino half)

**Color**
- Almost black room. One warm bulb. Metal goes Gunmetal, not chrome.
- Wood is dark and oily, not honey oak.
- Blood and neon are not the look. Dirt, grease, and one hot lamp are.

**Camera**
- Locked-off shot. Table fills the frame. Little camera move.
- Cylinder sits center, holes facing you. You read eight mouths in a ring.
- Hands and props sit in the same plane — flat, readable, not cinematic depth.

**Type**
- Big, blocky display for the title only. Short words.
- Numbers look stamped or mono, not fancy.
- Body text is plain and small. No glowing UI chrome.

**Table / chamber language**
- The cylinder is a machine part on a bench, not a prop on a casino felt.
- Chambers are empty dark holes or one hot fill. Empty ≠ Patina green wash across the UI — Patina only marks an empty chamber.
- Click = metal tick. No whoosh, no bounce.

**What is cliché here (skip it)**
- Green felt, poker chips, card suits as decoration, gold “luxury” trim, spinning wheel, neon signage, blood spray, horror scream faces.

### Inscryption (paper cards)

**Color**
- Stained paper, not bright white. Ours: Worn paper `#C8BFA8` on Soot.
- Ink is black. One metal accent (Brass `#B88A2E`), used like a wax seal or corner rivet.
- Cabin wood and soot around the cards — cards feel left on a desk, not floating in a store UI.

**Camera**
- Straight-on card. Orthographic. You see the whole face.
- Slight paper curl or deckle edge is enough. No 3D card flip circus.
- Cards sit in a row or a tight fan that does not hide names. Names live in the HUD, not only in the art.

**Type**
- Stamped / linocut feel on the card art.
- Player-facing names: clean sans in the DOM (IBM Plex Sans). Do not trust tiny banner text inside the picture.

**Card language**
- One clear picture. One clear job. HawkEye sees. Blocker holds. Healer mends.
- Edges look cut by hand (deckle). Corners can take a Brass rivet.
- Seal marks are workshop stamps (wax, 8-hole ring) — not ships’ anchors, not poker pips.

**What is cliché here (skip it)**
- Occult gore, dripping candles as the whole brand, “mystic glow,” anime eyes, glossy holographic foil, casino chip stacks on the card.

### How the two fit ROLET

| Steal from Buckshot | Steal from Inscryption | ROLET lock |
|---------------------|------------------------|------------|
| Dark bench, one lamp, metal tick | Paper stock, linocut face, stamp | Table v3 + paper cards + duelist as card art |
| Eight holes you can count | Three roles you can read | Chamber state in DOM; card names in DOM |
| No casino floor | No horror carnival | Stil v1.2 bans |
