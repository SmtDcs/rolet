# ROLET landing BG — cabinet brief (EN)

Empty workshop. Closed room. Ajar cabinet. Cylinder hides inside. Not a product shot of a giant cylinder on the table.

## 1. Composition
- Foreground: bare wood workbench, clean, almost empty (maybe one Brass rivet dish or coiled cable at the far edge — sparse).
- Mid: dark metal or wood cabinet / drawer bank against the back wall.
- One door cracked open on a diagonal. From the gap: half of an 8-chamber revolver cylinder, recessed, not hero-framed.
- Flicker light leaks from that gap onto the table edge and door lip.
- Negative space above the table for landing type (Soot void).

## 2. Camera
- Eye-level, slight low angle toward the cabinet.
- Locked off. 16:9. Table occupies lower third; cabinet upper-mid.
- Cylinder is small in frame — mystery, not the subject.
- No Dutch angle, no fisheye, no orbit.

## 3. Light
- Single practical: warm sodium flicker from inside the cabinet (`#D9963A`), pulsing once per beat, not strobe.
- Rest of room falls to Soot `#141414`.
- Soft contact shadow under the table lip. No overhead fluorescent bar. No neon.

## 4. Materials
- Table: oiled wood `#3A2A1F`, worn, matte.
- Cabinet: Gunmetal `#1E2227` / Graphite `#2E333A`, scratched paint, Brass `#B88A2E` hinge or latch only.
- Cylinder (half-seen): Gunmetal steel, eight bored holes, Brass hub — dull, workshop, light rust OK, not chrome.
- Air: light grain only. Paper/wood/metal. No felt.

## 5. Do not
- Giant cylinder centered on the table (rejected — looks like décor / catalog photo).
- Clutter piles, tools avalanche, blood, skulls.
- Casino felt, chips, neon, purple glow, emoji, UI chrome in the render.
- Cylinder filling the frame or facing camera as hero prop.
- Bright white fill light; cheerful workshop.
